### Important source limits
- OpenAI's reported team throughput, reduced engineering time and per-worktree observability are **author-reported from their environment**, not achievable performance estimates for our setup.
- SWE-Skills-Bench is a preprint with specific tasks, prompts and evaluation design; its aggregate numbers are not proof that adding/editing our skills hurts or helps. Use the **method of paired outcome checks**, not the headline as a policy.
- fast-check, Schemathesis and ast-grep are **tool projects**, not proof that adopting them will fix a specific ticket. No install approved.
- MCP servers / plugin creation / the proposed viewer UI are explicitly **parked for the later tooling workstream**. Only the existing evidence/result *contract* is in scope now.
